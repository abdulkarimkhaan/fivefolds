FiveFold – The Letter Duel

Privacy Policy · Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the FiveFold app for iPhone uses and why. FiveFold is published by the developer named on its App Store page. Questions: sharpmindgameapps@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress and statistics (Daily streak, results, settings). These stay on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Whether you've bought Remove Ads. Apple handles the purchase; we never see your payment details.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple shares your Game Center display name and player ID with the game so you can play other people online. Online matches send only game moves (letter colours and round results) between the players' devices through Apple's servers. There is no chat. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

FiveFold is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when asked
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions, app usage and diagnostic information

Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • You can allow or deny tracking at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the EU, the UK and other regions where it applies, you can review or change your ad consent at any time in FiveFold › Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Buying Remove Ads stops all ad breaks and makes hints free, with no video.

Data we collect ourselves

None. We don't run our own servers, and we don't collect, store or sell your personal information.

Children

FiveFold is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best made through the choices above or directly with Google. You can always email us for help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact and support

Email sharpmindgameapps@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
