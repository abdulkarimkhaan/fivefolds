FiveFold Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

FiveFold doesn't ask for your name, email or any account. Here's everything the app uses, in plain words.

Saved on your phone

Your scores, streak and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Online duels (Game Center)

If you play online, Apple's Game Center shares your Game Center name so your opponent can see who they're playing. Only game moves are sent between phones. There is no chat.

Ads

FiveFold is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use:

  • your phone's advertising ID, only if you tap Allow when FiveFold asks to track
  • your rough location, based on your internet connection
  • how you interact with ads

Read more in Google's explanation.

Your choices

  • Change tracking any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the EU and UK, change your ad choices in FiveFold › Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Buy Remove Ads to turn off ad breaks.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't have servers, and we don't collect or sell any personal information.

Children

FiveFold is made for a general audience, not specifically for children under 13.
